1. Introduction

Mornivo (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is an AI-powered calorie and nutrition tracker. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We’ve kept it short and specific — no generic legalese padding.

The short version: we collect the minimum needed to let you log meals, analyze food photos with AI, sync across devices, and process subscriptions. We don’t sell your data, don’t run ad networks, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

2. Information we collect

Account & identity

  • Anonymous user ID — assigned automatically on first launch so you can use the app without signing up.
  • Email address — only if you create an account or sign in with Google.
  • Authentication tokens — issued by our auth provider (Supabase) and stored securely on your device.

Health & activity data

  • Meal logs — food items, calories, macros (protein, carbs, fat), and meal type you record.
  • Food photos — images you take or upload for AI analysis. Stored in our cloud bucket so they can be re-displayed in your history.
  • Exercise & water intake — what you log manually.
  • Goals — daily targets you set (calories, macros).

Usage & technical data

  • AI credit usage — a per-account counter so we can enforce free-tier limits.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active Pro entitlement, managed by RevenueCat.
  • Device platform — iOS or Android, used to deliver platform-correct features.

What we don’t collect: we do not collect your precise location, contacts, calendar, microphone audio, or browsing history. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. How we use information

  • To analyze meal photos and return nutrition estimates via our AI provider.
  • To save your meal history and sync it across the devices you sign in on.
  • To enforce free-tier AI credit limits and unlock Pro features for paid subscribers.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Who we share with

We only share the minimum data needed with the following processors:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) — stores your meal logs, activity logs, food images, and account credentials. Supabase privacy.
  • Groq (AI provider) — receives the food photo and a short text prompt to return nutrition estimates. Images are processed transiently. Groq privacy.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — receives your anonymous user ID and purchase receipts to validate Pro entitlement. RevenueCat privacy.
  • Google Sign-In (optional) — if you sign in with Google, your Google email is shared with us. Google privacy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payments for subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card or banking details.

We may also disclose information if required by law (subpoena, court order) or to protect the rights and safety of users.

5. Retention & deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete everything at any time from inside the app:

  • Open Profile → Clear My Data to permanently delete all meals, photos, and reset your AI credits.
  • To delete your entire account (including email and authentication record), email us at the address below and we’ll process it within 30 days.

Anonymous sessions that have never been signed up are automatically purged after a period of inactivity.

6. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Database access is protected by row-level security so users can only read and write their own rows. Passwords are hashed by our auth provider (bcrypt), and authentication tokens are stored in your device’s secure storage. No system is ever 100% impenetrable, but we treat security failures as the most serious class of bug.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict how we process it.
  • Receive a portable copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these, email us — we’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re in the EU/UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Mornivo is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we make material changes, we’ll update the “Last updated” date at the top, and — for significant changes — show an in-app notice the next time you open Mornivo.
